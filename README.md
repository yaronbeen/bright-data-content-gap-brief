# Content Gap Brief

Check whether your page already answers a reader's question before commissioning another article.

Your agent uses [Bright Data](https://brightdata.com) for one bounded search and collects your page plus up to three relevant article bodies. It compares what the passages actually explain, not whether they contain identical keywords. The result is a focused editorial decision, not an invented SEO opportunity.

## What You Get

- A small question-coverage comparison with cited body passages.
- One writer brief with an outline and clear acceptance checks when a gap is supported.
- An already-covered decision or missing-evidence handoff when a new brief isn't justified.

## Give This To Your Agent

```text
Use gap-to-writer-assignment for [topic/question and reader task] and
[my article URL]. Use one query and one result page through my configured
Bright Data search tool, then collect my page and up to three relevant
article bodies through Bright Data. Compare question coverage by meaning.
Return one cited writer brief, an already-covered decision, or a
missing-evidence review. Keep exact quotes, source URLs, capture times,
and unknowns. If Bright Data is not connected, ask me to connect it and
stop. Do not invent traffic figures or publish anything.
```
